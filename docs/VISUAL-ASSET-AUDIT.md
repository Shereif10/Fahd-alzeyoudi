**Licence evidence:** the Unsplash License page (https://unsplash.com/license) states that images may be used "including for commercial purposes, without permission from or attributing the photographer or Unsplash". Restrictions: images cannot be sold without significant modification or compiled into a competing service. Neither applies here. Each photo was confirmed through Unsplash's own data as `premium: false, plus: false` (i.e. not Unsplash+). Sourced 10 Oct 2026. Credits are also recorded in `src/data/images.ts`.

**Selection criteria applied:** contemporary or heritage UAE architecture first (5 of 8 are Dubai-located); calm navy, blue-gray and stone tones matching `#293545 / #486479 / #97B5C9 / #E6E5E1`; no people, gavels, scales, courtrooms, handshakes or luxury black/gold; no filters applied. Two article images (financial towers, office corridor) are location-neutral, and their alt text does not claim a UAE location. Rejected: a Tokyo construction image and images with unknown location for the hero.

**Implementation (no design change):** only the existing `ArchImage` `src`/`alt` props are used; aspect ratios, the sheared-plane clip, the hero veil, grain overlay and crop CSS are unchanged. Files are WebP; `next/image` serves responsive sizes (verified HTTP 200 for all eight through the image optimiser). Alt text is localized EN/AR; the hero image stays decorative (`alt=""`) because the headline carries the meaning. Articles also publish the image as `og:image` and in `Article` JSON-LD.

**Bug fixed during image QA (pre-existing, not a design change):** every `data-reveal="clip"` element (architectural images and portrait placeholders, site-wide) stayed permanently clipped, because an element hidden by its own `clip-path` never reports an intersection. `RevealObserver` now observes the nearest ancestor that has a layout box and reveals the image through it. The animation itself is unchanged.

## 3. Icon usage

- **Used on the site: none.** The implemented design has no icon slots. Contact details are typographic label/value rows; cards and headings carry no icons.
- **Non-brand icons present:** only `Arrow` (UI glyph in the approved button/link system). No Lucide, Font Awesome, Material Icons or emoji.
- **FIGMA ICON SLOT VERIFICATION REQUIRED:** Figma access was not authorised in this session. If the approved Figma shows icon slots, the only candidates with a real purpose are Group 1 (WhatsApp, once the number exists), Groups 16 and 58 (LinkedIn/Instagram, once URLs exist) and Groups 17, 13/21, 48 (location, phone, email on office rows).
- **Intentionally unused:** all others. They are decorative, generic UI or off-topic (CSS, router, coffee cup, factory, camera, star, heart and similar). Group 14 is not used because the text switcher "EN / عربي" is clearer. Group 19 is not used on practice cards, where it would add 14 identical decorative icons.
