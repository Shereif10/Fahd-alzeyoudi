## 1. Civil Claims & Financial Claims
- **Relevant legislation:** Federal Decree-Law 25/2025 (CTL); Federal Decree-Law 42/2022 (CPL) as amended by 22/2025; Federal Decree-Law 35/2022 (Evidence).
- **Federal / local:** Federal substantive law. Forum is local (Dubai, AD, RAK) or federal (incl. Ajman), depending on the emirate.
- **Current status:** CTL in force 1 June 2026. CPL amendment in force 1 Jan 2026.
- **Relevant articles:** see X2, X3. Payment orders: CPL Part 11 (Arts. 143–144 per Lexis index). **VERIFY**. 2021-era payment-order appeal threshold AED 50,000 and 30-day window: **NOT CONFIRMED under 2022/2025 law. Kept off the public page.**
- **Amendments reviewed:** CTL replacement (2025); CPL 22/2025.
- **Official sources:** uaelegislation.gov.ae (portal); u.ae federal judiciary page.
- **Key findings:** transitional application is the main practical issue in 2026 claims. Moral damages are expressly recognised. Agreed damages can be reduced.
- **Jurisdiction:** DIFC/ADGM excluded. Small-claims procedures not researched (none found in the 2025 amendment summary).
- **Legal review items:** confirm CTL article numbers in Arabic official text; confirm current payment-order conditions and appeal period.

## 5. Construction & Contracting Disputes
- **Legislation:** CTL 25/2025, muqawala chapter (reported Arts. 812–839: **VERIFY**). Old-law decennial liability: Arts. 880–883 (1985). Art. 829 (new law): lump-sum price and exceptional circumstances (Chambers).
