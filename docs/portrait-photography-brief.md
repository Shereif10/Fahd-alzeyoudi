# Team portrait brief

The firm asked for a recommended look for team portraits before booking a professional shoot (Brief Q13). This specification matches the website's design and the monochrome photography style of the Brand Guidelines.

- **Format:** vertical 4:5. Deliver at least 2000 × 2500 px, uncropped, plus the RAW files.
- **Framing:** head and upper torso, eye line in the upper third. Leave space above the head. For the founder, also shoot a wider three-quarter-length frame for the leadership feature.
- **Background:** plain warm light gray, as close to brand `#E6E5E1` as possible, or a softly defocused architectural interior of the office. Every person should have the same background.
- **Light:** soft directional key light from one side at about 45°, with gentle fill. Avoid hard flash and glamour lighting.
- **Grade:** the site shows portraits in black and white with light grain. Supply colour originals; the site applies the grayscale.
- **Attire:** formal business dress in navy, charcoal or white, or national dress. Avoid busy patterns and bright colours.
- **Pose and expression:** calm and direct, with the body slightly angled and the face toward the camera. Approachable, not stern ("calm confidence", "human" in the brand character).
- **Consistency:** the same lens, height, distance and light for all nine team members.
- **Naming:** `firstname-lastname.jpg`, matching the profile URL (e.g. `Abanoub-aouni.jpg`).
